Free bots for Grok Bot

Two small bots for a kinder internet

Plus One Back helps you answer anger with attention. Got Your Back is a warm coach that checks in once a week.

Meet the bots

Two promises

  1. 01A human approves every reply.
  2. 02Always loving, never provocative.

The bots suggest. You decide.

The bots

Pick one, or use both

Both are free templates for Grok Bot. Each takes a few minutes of your time.

01

Plus One Back

Why it matters. Anger online is loud, and it's easy to answer it with more anger. One calm, curious reply helps someone feel heard, and shows everyone reading along that there is another way.

What it does. Once a day it finds one post from someone who is angry or afraid, and suggests a short, kind reply with an open question. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

02

Got Your Back

Why it matters. Change is easier when someone is in your corner. A warm check-in each week helps you see your progress and keep going.

What it does. Once a week it asks what went well, what you're struggling with and what you're afraid of. Then it helps you pick one small next step.
